## Data

### Synthetic phantoms (main experiment)
Generated by `src/reconstruction_mismatch/data/synthetic.py` (written for this project).
They are geometric test images, **not** cells, tissue or medical images.

## Models

### Small residual CNN denoiser (main experiment)
Architecture, training code and weights produced in this repository
(`src/reconstruction_mismatch/denoisers/cnn.py`, `train.py`), trained only on synthetic
training phantoms. The trained weights are committed as `results/checkpoints/den-2df14010572a_model.pt`
(sha256 `dade27cb429ef05a1331f3bcc106c9d55b926c96ec6ff3bb6b5714246a5ba1a2`, MIT like the rest of the
project). Design follows the published ideas of residual learning (DnCNN:
K. Zhang et al., IEEE TIP 2017) and a noise-level input map (FFDNet: K. Zhang, W. Zuo,
L. Zhang, IEEE TIP 2018); no code from those projects is used.

## Algorithms (described in papers, implemented here from the equations)
- Tikhonov regularisation, closed-form FFT solution for circular convolution.
- Plug-and-Play ADMM: S. V. Venkatakrishnan, C. A. Bouman, B. Wohlberg, "Plug-and-Play
  priors for model based reconstruction", GlobalSIP 2013; S. H. Chan, X. Wang, O. A. Elgendy,
  "Plug-and-Play ADMM for Image Restoration: Fixed-Point Convergence and Applications",
  IEEE TCI 3(1), 2017.
- SSIM: Z. Wang et al., IEEE TIP 2004, via `skimage.metrics.structural_similarity`.
